PoOlympics

Ragdoll athletes, learned from scratch

Application ID com.punkoutersoftware.poolympic
Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

PoOlympics does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no server of ours for the app to talk to. Two optional online features, both off by default, are described below.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information — no name, email, phone number or address.
  • It does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs or location.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.
  • It shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases.
  • It has no chat, no user accounts, no leaderboards and no way to share anything with other players.
  • It sends nothing to Punkouter Software, and nothing to any third party unless you switch on one of the optional features below.

What stays on your device

PoOlympics may save ordinary game state — progress, settings, results — in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, nobody else can read it, and uninstalling the app deletes it. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf because we never receive it in the first place.

Optional online features

Two features can reach the network, and both are off unless you switch them on in the game's settings. Neither sends anything about you.

  • AI commentary. Only works with your own Anthropic API key, which you supply. When on, the game sends the current heat's standings and one line of plain commentary to Anthropic's API and shows the reworded line it returns. Anthropic's own privacy policy covers that request.
  • Real weather. When on, the game asks Open-Meteo (a free weather service) for today's weather in the meet's fictional host city, once per session. The request contains only that city's coordinates; your own location is never read or sent.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission, used only by the optional features above. With them off, PoOlympics makes no network requests while you play it. If a future version genuinely needs the network for a feature, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Children

PoOlympics is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data it does not knowingly collect anything from children of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play listing as well.
